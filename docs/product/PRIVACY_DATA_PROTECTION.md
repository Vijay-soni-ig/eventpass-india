# Privacy & Data Protection

Data categories include identity/contact data, event registration, ticket/order records, payment references, check-in records, leads/interactions and operational audit data.

Principles: data minimization, purpose limitation, access control, retention, secure transport/storage, safe logging and controlled deletion/export where applicable.

Controls: retention schedule, secure backups, incident response, vendor/data-processor review and privacy notices/consent where required.

India-first launch requires review against applicable Indian law and any other operating jurisdictions. This document is not legal advice.
