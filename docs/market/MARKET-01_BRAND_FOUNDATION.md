# MARKET-01: ExhibitTix Brand Foundation

**Status:** Draft for implementation  
**Workstream:** Market Track  
**Owner:** ExhibitTix  
**Source of truth:** Product capabilities verified in the repository on 2026-09-27

## 1. Objective

Establish a clear, organizer-first brand foundation for ExhibitTix before outbound marketing begins.

The brand must communicate that ExhibitTix is not only an event discovery and ticketing product. It is an operating platform for organizers to plan, configure, sell, run, and measure exhibitions and events.

This document is the messaging source of truth for the website, LinkedIn, outreach, sales demos, beta recruitment, and future marketing assets.

## 2. Current product reality

The repository currently describes and implements a broad event lifecycle covering:

- Event creation and management
- Venue, hall, and stall management
- Exhibitor applications and participation
- Stall selection, reservations, payments, and concurrency protection
- Visitor registration and ticketing
- QR ticketing and check-in
- Lead capture and exhibitor analytics
- Payments, refunds, invoices, fees, taxes, subscriptions, and auditability
- Role-based access for Super Admin, Organizer, Exhibitor, and Visitor

The current public-facing homepage is primarily visitor-oriented. Its core message is "Discover Exhibitions Near You", with supporting messages around discovery, payments, QR tickets, and check-in.

That positioning is valid for the visitor product, but it does not adequately communicate the organizer-first SaaS business model.

## 3. Positioning

### Category

**Exhibition and event operations platform for organizers.**

### Primary audience

Exhibition and event organizers who need to manage the operational lifecycle of an event across exhibitors, venues, stalls, visitors, payments, check-in, leads, and reporting.

### Secondary audiences

- Exhibition companies and agencies
- Trade-fair organizers
- Venue-led event teams
- Professional conference and event organizers
- Exhibitors, as downstream users of organizer-managed events
- Visitors, as downstream event attendees

### Positioning statement

> ExhibitTix helps exhibition and event organizers run the operational lifecycle of their events in one platform, from event setup and exhibitor participation to stall bookings, ticketing, check-in, lead capture, and analytics.

## 4. Core brand promise

**Run the event, not a pile of disconnected tools.**

This is a strategic message, not a claim that every external tool is replaced immediately. Marketing copy must remain consistent with verified product capability.

## 5. Primary messaging hierarchy

### Message 1: Organizer outcome

**Run exhibitions and events from one platform.**

Supporting copy:

> Set up events, manage exhibitors and stalls, sell tickets, check in visitors, capture leads, and understand event performance from one operational system.

### Message 2: Operational control

**From event setup to post-event analytics.**

The lifecycle should be shown visually:

**Event → Venue/Hall → Exhibitors → Stalls → Payments → Visitors → Tickets → QR Check-in → Leads → Analytics**

### Message 3: Business value

Emphasize operational visibility and reduced fragmentation rather than unsupported quantitative claims.

Approved themes:

- One operational workspace
- Centralized event data
- Faster event administration
- Better visibility into exhibitors and visitors
- Digital ticketing and check-in
- Lead capture connected to event participation
- Financial and operational records in one system

Do not claim specific percentage improvements, customer counts, revenue generated, or time saved unless verified by evidence.

## 6. Tagline direction

### Primary working tagline

**Run your event. Grow your exhibition.**

### Product descriptor

**Exhibition & Event Management Platform**

### Alternative campaign lines

These are campaign options, not competing product definitions:

- **Everything behind a successful event, in one place.**
- **From event setup to visitor check-in.**
- **Plan. Sell. Run. Measure.**
- **The operating platform for modern exhibitions.**

The primary tagline should remain stable while campaign lines can change by audience and channel.

## 7. Audience-specific messaging

### Organizer

Primary pain:

Fragmented event operations across spreadsheets, forms, payment systems, messaging tools, ticketing tools, and manual processes.

Message:

> Manage your event operations in one place, from setup and exhibitors to ticketing, check-in, leads, and analytics.

CTA:

**Join the ExhibitTix beta**

Secondary CTA:

**Talk to the team**

### Exhibitor

Message:

> Manage your exhibition participation, stall booking, payments, team, and visitor leads from one place.

CTA:

**Explore exhibitor tools**

### Visitor

The existing visitor proposition remains:

> Discover exhibitions and events, book tickets, receive a QR ticket, and check in digitally.

CTA:

**Explore events**

### Enterprise / larger organizers

Do not market advanced enterprise capabilities until verified.

Use:

> Talk to us about your event operations.

Avoid claiming enterprise SSO, multi-country, advanced CRM, or other roadmap-only capabilities.

## 8. Brand voice

ExhibitTix should sound:

- Clear
- Practical
- Confident
- Modern
- Professional
- Operational
- Helpful

Avoid:

- "Revolutionary"
- "Game-changing"
- "World-class" without evidence
- "India's #1" without independent evidence
- Fake customer statistics
- Unsupported ROI percentages
- Excessive SaaS jargon
- Generic AI-generated marketing language

### Writing rule

Lead with the organizer's operational problem and outcome. Describe features only after establishing why they matter.

Bad:

> ExhibitTix provides powerful AI-driven next-generation event management solutions.

Better:

> Manage exhibitors, stalls, tickets, check-in, and leads from one event workspace.

## 9. Visual brand direction

The existing product visual system provides a usable foundation:

- Primary hue: teal
- Neutral foundation: light gray / white
- Typography: Manrope for display, Inter for body
- Rounded cards and restrained shadows
- Professional SaaS UI language
- Accessible focus states
- Responsive layouts
- Existing dark-mode support

### Visual direction

Keep the existing teal identity rather than introducing a new color system during MARKET-01.

Evolve the brand through:

1. Stronger organizer-oriented imagery
2. More confident typography hierarchy
3. Event-operation visuals such as floor plans, dashboards, ticket scans, exhibitor lists, and analytics
4. Consistent use of the ExhibitTix logo and wordmark
5. Clear separation between organizer-product messaging and visitor-discovery messaging

Do not redesign the entire application during MARKET-01.

## 10. Website information architecture direction

The public website should eventually make the two sides of ExhibitTix obvious:

### For organizers

- Platform overview
- Event management
- Exhibitor management
- Stall management
- Ticketing
- Check-in
- Lead capture
- Analytics
- Pricing
- Beta / demo CTA

### For visitors

- Discover events
- Event details
- Ticket booking
- My tickets
- QR check-in information

### For exhibitors

- Participation
- Stall booking
- Payments
- Leads
- Performance

The organizer acquisition path should not depend on visitors discovering the product first.

## 11. Primary acquisition CTA

Until the platform is fully production-ready:

**Join the ExhibitTix beta**

Supporting text:

> We're working with a small group of organizers to shape the platform around real event operations.

This is preferable to a generic "Sign Up" CTA because it matches the current pre-launch stage and creates an explicit beta pipeline.

Secondary CTA:

**Book a demo**

## 12. Proof strategy

Before public marketing claims are expanded, build proof in this order:

1. Product screenshots
2. Verified workflows
3. Organizer discovery interviews
4. Beta organizers
5. Real event case studies
6. Quantified outcomes only after measurement
7. Testimonials with permission

Never manufacture proof.

## 13. Brand claims policy

Every public claim must be classified as:

- **Verified:** directly supported by the current product
- **Beta:** available to selected beta users but not necessarily production-wide
- **Roadmap:** planned but not currently available
- **Marketing opinion:** subjective positioning language

Only Verified and appropriately qualified Beta claims should be presented as current product capability.

Roadmap features must not be presented as shipped functionality.

## 16. Important product/market decision

ExhibitTix should **not** abandon its visitor-facing experience.

The correct architecture is:

**Organizer-first business model + multi-sided product experience**

Organizers are the primary customer and acquisition target.

Exhibitors and visitors are critical platform participants whose experience increases the value of the organizer product.

This distinction should guide future marketing, pricing, navigation, onboarding, and sales strategy.
