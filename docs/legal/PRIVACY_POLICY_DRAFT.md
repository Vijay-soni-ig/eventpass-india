# ExhibitTix Privacy Policy Draft

**DRAFT — legal review required**

ExhibitTix may process account, organizer, exhibitor, visitor, registration, ticketing, payment-reference, check-in, lead and operational data to provide and secure the platform.

Use includes account management, event operations, ticketing, payments, check-in, lead management, support, security, analytics and legally permitted communications.

Access is restricted by role and tenant scope. Payment credentials should be handled by the configured payment provider rather than stored unnecessarily by ExhibitTix.

Retention, user rights, processor disclosures, transfers, cookies, marketing consent, jurisdiction, controller/processor roles and contact details must be finalized before publication.
